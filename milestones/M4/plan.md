# M4/M5 execution plan

**Written:** 1 November 2026, before any M4 aggregation, ablation, or failure
analysis was computed. Source of truth: `../M3/hypothesis.md` (decision rule)
and `../M3/experiment_design.md` (M4 specification). Neither file, nor anything
in `../../results/baseline/`, is edited by any job below.

**One intervention:** Patient-ID–stratified re-aggregation of the frozen M2
out-of-fold (OOF) predictions. No retraining, threshold change, new data,
augmentation, or model change. "Patient-ID" always means the official NLM
mapping key in `patient_id`.

**Number discipline:** every number in a results table, the paper, the slides,
or the milestone write-ups is produced by code from files under `results/`.
Markdown write-ups are rendered from `*.tmpl.md` templates by
`python -m src.render_reports`; the paper reads `\val{key}` macros and tables
generated by `paper/make_tables.py`. Both fail if a key is missing.

## Decision rule constants (copied from `../M3/hypothesis.md`)

- Primary metric: per-fold macro-by-Patient-ID sensitivity (IDs with ≥ 1
  Parasitized cell) minus pooled cell-level sensitivity, in percentage points.
- SUPPORTED: ≥ 4 of 5 differences negative **and** mean ≤ −1.68 pp.
- UNSUPPORTED: ≥ 4 of 5 differences positive **and** mean ≥ +1.68 pp.
- INCONCLUSIVE: otherwise, including any failed reuse assertion or
  unavailable denominators.

The threshold is applied as written (1.68 pp). Its exact source value,
half of the M2 sensitivity fold SD (3.358… / 2 = 1.679… pp), is reported
alongside, together with whether the verdict would differ under it.
